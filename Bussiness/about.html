SHABRE EXPORTS

Growing Opportunities Through Agriculture

Shabre Exports is the trading brand of Shelter of Good Hope Agro Enterprises Ltd., a Ugandan company involved in agricultural products and supply.

The company began operations in 2018 on a small scale and has continued to grow by building relationships with farmers, cooperatives, suppliers and customers.

We focus on connecting quality agricultural products from Uganda with customers and businesses looking for reliable agricultural supplies.

OUR STORY

From a Small Beginning to Growing Markets

Shelter of Good Hope Agro Enterprises Ltd. began operations in 2018 with a focus on creating market opportunities for agricultural producers.

The company was established to help connect agricultural producers with markets and to create opportunities for Ugandan agricultural products to reach more customers.

Since then, the company has continued to build relationships with farmer cooperatives and individual growers while developing supply networks for different customers and markets.
